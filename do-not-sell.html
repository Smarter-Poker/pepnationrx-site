Your right to opt out

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (together, the "CCPA"), gives you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information. "Sharing" includes disclosures to third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising, even when no money changes hands.

Pep Nation Rx does not sell your personal information for money. We also do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising at this time. If that ever changes — for example, if we add an advertising pixel or audience-matching tool to this site — your submission on this page will be honored as an opt-out from any such sharing on the device, browser, and account you identify.

Submitting this form does not stop the disclosures we need to make to deliver care, such as routing your intake to a licensed clinician or sending your prescription to a licensed pharmacy. Those are described in our Privacy Policy and, where applicable, the Notice of Privacy Practices.

What information is covered

Your opt-out applies to categories of personal information we may collect, including: identifiers (such as name, email, IP address); commercial information (such as products or plans you view); internet or other electronic network activity (such as pages visited and device data); and inferences drawn from those categories. We do not knowingly use sensitive personal information — including the health information you submit during intake — for targeted advertising.

Other ways to opt out

  • Global Privacy Control: If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we will treat it as a valid opt-out for that browser without requiring this form.
  • Cookie preferences: You can turn off Analytics and Marketing categories at any time from Cookie preferences.
  • Authorized agent: You may use an authorized agent to submit this request on your behalf, subject to verification.

Submit an opt-out request

Use this form to record your opt-out for this browser and any account that matches the email you provide. We will acknowledge receipt and act on your request as soon as feasibly possible, and in any case within 45 days of receipt. We may extend the response window by up to an additional 45 days when reasonably necessary and we will tell you if we do.

We respond within 45 days.

Request received

Thank you. We have logged your opt-out for this browser. If you provided an email, we will send a confirmation and follow up if we need to verify your identity. You will hear from us within 45 days of receipt.

If we deny your request

If we are unable to verify your identity or cannot honor your request for a permitted reason, we will tell you why and how to appeal. To appeal, reply to our response email or contact us using the details below within 30 days.

Contact and complaint

Privacy requests and complaints can be sent to [privacy@pepnationrx.com] or to [Pep Nation Rx, attn: Privacy Officer, street, city, state, ZIP]. California residents may also contact the California Privacy Protection Agency at cppa.ca.gov or the California Attorney General at oag.ca.gov/privacy.

See also our Privacy Policy, Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, and Notice of Privacy Practices.
