Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026 · Effective on launch

Working draft

This document is a structural draft. Final privacy language — including HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, state-specific disclosures, and your verified data practices — must be reviewed and approved by qualified counsel and your privacy officer before the site goes live.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Pep Nation Rx collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website and use our consumer services. It applies to information processed by Pep Nation Rx as a technology and management platform; it does not govern protected health information held by treating clinicians or pharmacies under HIPAA, which is addressed separately below.

2. Information You Provide

We collect information you submit directly, such as your name, contact details, account credentials, payment information, and the health and intake information you provide so it can be routed to a licensed clinician.

3. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the site, we automatically collect limited technical data such as your device type, browser, IP address, pages viewed, and interactions, which we use to operate and secure the Service.

4. Information From Other Sources

We may receive information about you from service providers, treating clinicians and pharmacies, payment processors, and analytics partners, which we combine with information you provide to deliver and improve the Service.

5. How We Use Information

We use information to operate the platform, route your intake to an appropriate licensed clinician, support billing and shipping, communicate with you about your account and care, secure the Service, comply with law, and improve our offerings.

6. How We Disclose / Share Information

We share information with the independent clinicians who review your intake, the licensed pharmacies that prepare and ship treatment, and service providers who support operations such as payment processing, delivery, and analytics. We may also disclose information to comply with law or in connection with a corporate transaction. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences such as your light or dark theme, measure performance, and support marketing. You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where available, opt-out mechanisms.

8. Your Privacy Choices & Rights

You may access and update account information, opt out of non-essential marketing communications, and adjust cookie preferences. Depending on your location, you may also have additional rights described below.

9. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards — including encryption in transit and at rest — designed to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors, and we will delete such information if we learn it has been collected.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Service and to meet legal, regulatory, and recordkeeping obligations, after which it is deleted or de-identified.

12. State Privacy Rights

Residents of California (under the CCPA/CPRA) and certain other states may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, and limit the use of personal information, and to opt out of certain sharing or targeted advertising. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights, and you may submit a request through the contact listed below. [Final list of covered states and the verified rights-request process to be confirmed by counsel.]

13. Relationship to HIPAA

This consumer Privacy Policy is not the HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices. Protected health information created or held in connection with your clinical care is governed by HIPAA and described in our Notice of Privacy Practices, which is owned by the affiliated professional medical group. Please review that notice for your rights regarding health information.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.

15. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to the contact listed on our help center. See also our Terms of Service, Legal Notices, and Notice of Privacy Practices.
