Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026 · Effective on launch

Working draft

This document is a structural draft. Final privacy language — including HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, state-specific disclosures, and your verified data practices — must be reviewed and approved by qualified counsel and your privacy officer before the site goes live.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Pep Nation Rx collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and services. We are committed to protecting your personal and health information.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, contact details, account credentials, and the health information you submit during intake — as well as limited technical data such as device and usage information needed to operate the site.

3. Protected health information and HIPAA

Health information you provide for clinical review is handled in accordance with applicable law. Where Pep Nation Rx acts as a business associate of treating clinicians or pharmacies, that information is governed by HIPAA and the applicable Notice of Privacy Practices.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to operate the platform, route your intake to an appropriate licensed clinician, support billing and shipping, communicate with you about your care and account, and improve and secure the Service.

5. How we share information

We share information with the independent clinicians who review your intake, the licensed pharmacies that prepare and ship your treatment, and service providers who support operations such as payment processing and delivery. We do not sell your personal or health information.

6. Data security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards — including encryption in transit and at rest — designed to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on your state of residence, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete certain personal information, and to opt out of certain uses. Requests can be made through the support contact on our help center.

8. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site and remember your preferences, such as your light or dark theme choice. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Data retention

We retain personal and health information for as long as needed to provide the Service and to meet legal, regulatory, and recordkeeping obligations, after which it is deleted or de-identified.

10. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to the contact listed on our help center.
