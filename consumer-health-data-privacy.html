Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026 · Effective on launch

Working draft

This document is a structural draft addressing the Washington My Health My Data Act and similar consumer health privacy frameworks. Final language — including the named entity, specific affiliates, contact details, and the verified data practices — must be reviewed and approved by qualified counsel and your privacy officer before the site goes live. Bracketed placeholders mark fields that must be confirmed.

1. Scope of this policy

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy describes how [Pep Nation Rx, LLC] ("Pep Nation Rx," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects consumer health data. It is provided to satisfy the Washington My Health My Data Act ("MHMDA") and to give consumers in any state a single, plain-language description of our consumer health data practices. It supplements, and is in addition to, our general Privacy Policy and our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

For purposes of this policy, "consumer health data" means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to a consumer and that identifies the consumer's past, present, or future physical or mental health status — for example, intake responses, conditions disclosed during a visit, medications, treatment history, biometric information, and precise location information that could reasonably indicate an attempt to receive health services.

2. Categories of consumer health data we collect

  • Identifying information tied to health context — full name, contact details, account credentials, and date of birth.
  • Intake and visit information — health history, symptoms, conditions, allergies, current medications, lifestyle questions, photos uploaded as part of intake, and your responses to the medical questionnaire.
  • Treatment and prescription information — medications prescribed, dosages, refills, shipping confirmation, and clinician notes you can see in your account.
  • Communications with the affiliated medical group, clinicians, pharmacies, and our support team that contain health-related information.
  • Device and usage information from your interactions with the site that, in context, can reveal that you sought or considered a particular health service.
  • Precise location information only where you provide it (such as shipping address) or where required to confirm we can lawfully serve your state.

We do not knowingly collect consumer health data from anyone under 18.

3. Categories of sources

  • Directly from you, when you create an account, complete intake, message support, or update your profile.
  • From the affiliated medical group and its independent clinicians, when they document the outcome of an asynchronous or synchronous visit.
  • From the licensed pharmacy that fulfills your prescription, for shipping status and refill eligibility.
  • From service providers that help us run the platform — for example, identity verification, payment processing, hosting, customer support, secure messaging, and analytics — strictly under contracts that limit their use of the data.
  • Automatically from your device, when you interact with the site, in the form of basic technical data such as device type, browser, IP, and pages viewed.

4. Purposes of collection and use

  • To connect you with an independent, licensed clinician and to support their clinical evaluation.
  • To enable the licensed pharmacy to prepare and ship any prescription that the clinician issues.
  • To create and secure your account, authenticate you, prevent fraud, and respond to support requests.
  • To process payment for the consultation and, where applicable, the medication.
  • To comply with applicable laws, including state telehealth, pharmacy, and recordkeeping requirements.
  • To improve safety, reliability, and performance of the site and the service.
  • To send service communications about your account or care, and — only with your consent — marketing communications that you can withdraw at any time.

We do not use consumer health data for targeted advertising, and we do not sell consumer health data. See Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information for the related CCPA opt-out.

5. Categories of consumer health data we share

We share only the categories described in section 2, and only the minimum needed for the purpose. Each category may be shared with one or more of the recipient categories listed in section 6 — for example, intake and visit information is shared with the affiliated medical group and treating clinician; treatment and prescription information is shared with the dispensing pharmacy and shipping carrier.

  • Shipping and logistics carriers for delivery of prescriptions, limited to the information needed to ship the package.
  • Professional advisors (legal, accounting, audit) and government or regulatory authorities when required by law, subpoena, or to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • Successors in a corporate transaction (such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale) under terms that continue the protections in this policy.

7. We do not sell consumer health data

We do not sell consumer health data for monetary or other valuable consideration, and we do not share consumer health data for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will not begin doing so without first obtaining the specific, separate, written authorization that the law requires, in a form that meets every element required by RCW 19.373.030 — including, where applicable, identifying the specific data, the recipient, the purpose, the right to revoke, and an effective and expiration date.

8. Your rights

If you are a Washington consumer, MHMDA gives you the following rights with respect to your consumer health data:

  • Right to confirm and access. You may ask whether we are collecting, sharing, or selling your consumer health data and request a copy, along with a list of the third parties and affiliates with which we have shared it.
  • Right to withdraw consent. You may withdraw consent for the collection and sharing of your consumer health data, going forward, for any processing that depends on your consent.
  • Right to deletion. You may ask us to delete consumer health data we have collected about you. We will also notify our processors and the affiliates and third parties with whom we shared the data, as required by law.

You may exercise these rights regardless of where you live; we describe the request process in the next section.

9. How to exercise your rights

You may submit a rights request by emailing [privacy@pepnationrx.com], by writing to [Pep Nation Rx, attn: Privacy Officer, street, city, state, ZIP], or by signing in to your account and using the privacy controls there. You may also designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf, subject to verification.

So that we can act on your request and protect your data, we will need to verify your identity using information already associated with your account, such as your email and a recent visit detail. We will respond within forty-five (45) days of receipt. We may extend that period once, by up to forty-five additional days, where reasonably necessary; if we do, we will tell you within the first forty-five days. There is no charge to submit a request.

10. Right to appeal

If we deny a rights request, you may appeal by replying to our response email or by writing to the contact above within thirty (30) days. We will explain in writing why we granted or denied the appeal within forty-five (45) days. If the appeal is denied, you may contact the Washington Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint to file a complaint, or contact the Attorney General in your state of residence.

11. Authorization required for sharing or selling

Before sharing consumer health data with a third party in a way that goes beyond what is necessary to provide the product or service you requested, we will obtain your separate, valid consent. Before any sale of consumer health data (which we do not currently engage in), we will obtain your separate, written authorization that meets every element required by law and may be revoked by you at any time.

12. Data security and retention

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect consumer health data — including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, audit logging, and security monitoring. We retain consumer health data only as long as needed for the purposes described above and to meet legal, regulatory, and clinical recordkeeping requirements, after which it is deleted or de-identified in accordance with those requirements.

13. Children

Our services are intended for adults eighteen (18) and older. We do not knowingly collect consumer health data from minors. If we learn that we have collected such data, we will delete it.

14. Contact and complaints

Privacy questions, requests, and complaints can be sent to [privacy@pepnationrx.com] or to [Pep Nation Rx, attn: Privacy Officer, street, city, state, ZIP]. Washington consumers may also file a complaint with the Washington Attorney General under the Consumer Protection Act at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date. If the changes are material, we will give you advance notice through the site or by email before they take effect, and we will not retroactively use consumer health data we already collected in materially different ways without obtaining the consent the law requires.

See also our Privacy Policy, Notice of Privacy Practices, and Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information.
