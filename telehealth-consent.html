Consent to Telehealth Services

Last updated 2026 · Effective on launch

Working draft

This document is a structural draft. Final telehealth-consent language must be reviewed and approved by qualified counsel and the affiliated medical group before the site goes live. In production, this consent is also presented as a required checkbox that you must affirmatively accept during intake before a clinician reviews your information.

1. What Telehealth Is

Telehealth is the delivery of health care using secure electronic communications — such as online questionnaires, messaging, audio, and video — to connect you with a licensed clinician who is in a different location than you. It allows evaluation, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, and follow-up to occur remotely.

2. Benefits of Telehealth

Telehealth can make care more convenient and accessible, reduce travel and wait times, and allow you to communicate with a licensed clinician from a private location. It may also support timely follow-up and ongoing management of certain conditions.

3. Limitations & Risks

Telehealth has limitations and risks, including the possibility of technology failures or delays, the absence of a hands-on physical examination, and the chance that information available remotely is insufficient for a clinician to make a decision. As a result, a clinician may be unable to diagnose or treat your condition remotely and may recommend in-person evaluation or follow-up.

4. Not a Substitute for In-Person or Emergency Care

Telehealth is not a substitute for in-person medical care and is not appropriate for medical emergencies. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.

5. Voluntary Consent & Withdrawal

Your decision to receive care through telehealth is voluntary. You may withhold or withdraw this consent at any time without affecting your right to future care, although doing so may end your ability to use this Service.

6. The Provider May Decline Treatment

An independent licensed clinician may, in their sole professional judgment, decline to provide treatment, request additional information, or refer you to in-person care. Submitting an intake does not guarantee a prescription or treatment plan.

7. Data Transmission & Security

Information you provide is transmitted and stored using administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect it, including encryption in transit and at rest. No method of electronic transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and there is a residual risk that communications could be intercepted or accessed without authorization.

8. Third-Party Beneficiary

You acknowledge that Pep Nation Rx, as the technology and management services organization supporting this Service, is named as an intended third-party beneficiary of this Consent to Telehealth Services and may rely on it. Pep Nation Rx is not a medical provider, and this consent does not create a provider-patient relationship with Pep Nation Rx.

9. Contact

Questions about this consent can be sent to the support contact listed on our help center. See also our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Legal Notices, and Notice of Privacy Practices.
