Notice of Privacy Practices (HIPAA)

Last updated 2026 · Effective on launch

Working draft

This document is a structural draft. A HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices is a regulated legal document of the covered entity. This draft must be owned, completed, and finalized by the affiliated professional medical group / MSO together with qualified counsel and a privacy officer — including the legal entity name, contact details, and verified data practices — before the site goes live. This consumer-facing draft is not the operative notice.

1. Our Commitment

This Notice describes how protected health information ("PHI") about you may be used and disclosed by the treating clinicians and affiliated medical group, and how you can access that information. We are required by law to maintain the privacy of your PHI, to provide this Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and to follow the terms of the Notice currently in effect.

2. How We May Use and Disclose Health Information

We may use and disclose your PHI for treatment — such as sharing information among clinicians and pharmacies to evaluate, prescribe, and coordinate your care; for payment — such as billing and collecting for services and verifying coverage; and for health care operations — such as quality review, training, and administrative activities needed to run the practice.

3. Other Permitted Uses

We may also use or disclose your PHI without your authorization when permitted or required by law — for example, for public health activities, health oversight, legal proceedings, law enforcement requests, to avert a serious threat to health or safety, or as otherwise required by federal or state law.

4. Uses Requiring Your Authorization

Other uses and disclosures — including most uses for marketing, any sale of PHI, and most disclosures of psychotherapy notes — will be made only with your written authorization. You may revoke an authorization in writing at any time, except to the extent we have already acted in reliance on it.

5. Your Health Information Rights

You have the right to request access to and a copy of your PHI, to request corrections or amendments, to receive an accounting of certain disclosures, to request restrictions on certain uses and disclosures, to request confidential communications, and to receive a paper copy of this Notice upon request.

6. Our Responsibilities

We are required to maintain the privacy and security of your PHI, to notify you following a breach of unsecured PHI, to follow the duties and privacy practices described in this Notice, and not to use or disclose your information other than as described here unless you tell us we may.

7. Changes to This Notice

We may change the terms of this Notice, and any change will apply to all PHI we maintain. The revised Notice will be posted with a new effective date and made available upon request.

8. Complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with us using the contact below, or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights. You will not be retaliated against for filing a complaint.

9. Contact

Privacy questions, requests to exercise your rights, and complaints can be directed to the privacy contact listed on our help center.
