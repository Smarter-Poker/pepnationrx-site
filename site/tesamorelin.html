Common questions

What is tesamorelin?

Tesamorelin is a synthetic analog of growth-hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) studied for visceral fat reduction in specific populations. It is prepared by a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy for an individual patient based on a clinician's prescription. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

When am I charged?

Only after a licensed clinician reviews your intake and approves a treatment plan. If you are not approved, you are not charged.
